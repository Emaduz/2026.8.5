# Current-site identity notes

The current public site is titled `Eng.EmadAlddine` and presents EmadAlddine as a Senior Graphic Designer. The main navigation is Home, Portfolio, About, Services, and Contact, with Arabic language toggle controls.

The visible homepage copy includes the hero headline `Creative Design Solutions`, the supporting line `Transforming ideas into impactful visual experiences with 9+ years of expertise`, calls to action `See My Work` and `Hire Me`, and the proof points `9+ Years Experience`, `200+ Happy Clients`, and `500+ Projects Completed`.

The featured work area includes Corporate Branding, Logo Design Collection, and Print Materials. The current social-proof area lists client/project names and statements; these are user-provided content from the existing site and should not be fabricated or expanded in the new app. The closing CTA is `Ready to Start Your Project?` with Contact and WhatsApp actions.

The footer identifies Eng.EmadAlddine as a Senior Graphic Designer, with `Creative graphic designer with 9+ years of experience in branding and visual identity development.`, contact email `info@emadalddine.com`, phone `+966 504487308`, location `Al-Madina, Saudi Arabia`, and links to Instagram, LinkedIn, and Behance.

The current rendered font family is `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`. Dominant computed colors are dark red `rgb(143, 24, 25)`, dark charcoal `rgb(45, 45, 45)`, warm brown `rgb(156, 120, 96)`, cream/tan `rgb(217, 202, 177)` / `rgba(217, 202, 177, 0.8)`, white `rgb(255, 255, 255)`, dusty coral `rgb(189, 123, 106)`, and pale cream `rgb(255, 249, 243)`. Borders use light neutral `rgb(229, 229, 229)`.
