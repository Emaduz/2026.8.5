# Isti Template Review

## Visual language

The template uses a dark near-black canvas with acidic lime accents, oversized condensed/display headings, large circular portrait composition, hard geometric background shapes, and strong horizontal section rhythm. Its navigation stays persistent at the top and links to Home, About, Services, Projects, Blog, and Contact; the homepage is a long editorial scroll rather than a set of separate full-page routes.

## Motion and transitions

The perceived motion comes from scroll-triggered reveal and clipping effects: headings reveal progressively, images fade or slide into view, section labels and controls animate into place, and the layout uses dark overlays, blur, and geometric masks. The page also has a back-to-top control and visible hover/interaction affordances. The motion is expressive but should be adapted carefully to the existing site's more refined warm editorial identity rather than copied with the lime/dark palette.

## Content order

The observed order is hero, About, Services, Skills/experience, Projects/gallery, testimonials, Blog, newsletter, and Contact. The projects section is visually separated as a gallery and uses a repeated image grid/portfolio showcase rather than a nested case-study carousel.

## Recommendation for emadalddine.com

Use the interaction model, not the template's colors: add scroll reveal, staggered text/image entrances, section masking, subtle image scale on entry, and smoother page transitions. Keep the current cream/oxblood/Georgia identity, keep dedicated pages, and adapt the projects area to the requested nested per-project carousel. Use a wider desktop grid with stronger left/right alignment and a persistent top navigation. Avoid copying the template's testimonials or placeholder copy, and keep all displayed project assets from the owner's Behance work.
