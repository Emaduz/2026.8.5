# Contact validation notes

The public `/contact` route now follows the reference structure with a centered Get In Touch hero, a two-column message form and contact details area, a profile card with portrait and response note, social links, and a red FAQ section. The desktop preview keeps the form and sidebar balanced, while the mobile preview stacks the form, contact card, profile card, social links, FAQ, and footer without horizontal overflow. The contact form is functional as a mailto handoff and preserves Arabic/English copy through the locale dictionary in the page component.
